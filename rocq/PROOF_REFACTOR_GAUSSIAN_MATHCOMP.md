## GaussianPosteriorMeasure.v — 3 proof scripts

| Proof script (kind) | Exact mathematical scope | Reused library | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gmc_sufficient_statistic_posterior_center` (theorem) | If the prior variance is `c` and sample-mean noise variance is `(1-c)/n`, the conjugate normal location is `c*S_n/d_n(c)` | MathComp-Analysis `post_mean`, MathComp `field_tactic` | Does not construct the observed n-vector |
| `gmc_sufficient_statistic_posterior_variance` (theorem) | The conjugate normal posterior squared standard deviation equals `c*(1-c)/d_n(c)` | MathComp-Analysis `post_stddevE` | Parameter algebra, not a second-moment integral |
| `gmc_normalized_gaussian_sufficient_statistic_posterior` (theorem) | Prior-normal likelihood integral on a measurable event divided by the evidence integral equals the **normalized** `normal_prob` measure with the report's location and posterior spread | MathComp-Analysis `normal_prob_conjugate` | Only the single normally distributed observed sample mean, not yet the full vector conditional expectation |

The last theorem is genuinely measure-theoretic: its left side is a ratio
of integrals against an existing normal **probability measure**, and its
right side is a normal probability measure. It must not be conflated
with the earlier unnormalized exponential-kernel identity.

## Remaining conditional-expectation obligation

Still required for the full report: (1) construct an n-dimensional
equicorrelated jointly Gaussian score vector, including negative
correlations allowed by positive definiteness; (2) establish that its
observed mean is a sufficient statistic for the common factor in the
positive-correlation latent model; (3) prove integrability, mean and
variance identities of the posterior distribution and future noise;
(4) verify the conditional-expectation universal property on the
sigma-algebra generated by all observed scores. Singular endpoints
require a separate non-inverse argument.

A normalized latent posterior for a summary observation makes concrete
progress but is **not** already a proof of
`E[X_future | sigma(X_observed)]` for all observations.
