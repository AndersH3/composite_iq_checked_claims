# D049x — unequal observed-history regression algebra (106 theorems)

**Author/researcher:** Anders Hellström.

> **AI disclosure:** An AI system authored the candidate Rocq statements, scripts, comments and this source-order register. Only a successful Rocq 9.2 kernel run on an exact commit establishes their theorem status. The 106 identities are related deterministic linear-forecast and calibration results, **not** 106 independent probabilistic laws.

These results remove the *equal initial observations* limitation of the existing algebraic candidate-forecast formulas: arbitrary observed scores enter through their total `s`. They express how a candidate predictor, future score total, inclusive score mean, prediction residual and baseline-centered report score respond to historical perturbations and future batches. They reuse `SequentialReport.sequential_d` and Rocq Stdlib Ring. The separately required Gaussian conditional-expectation theorem, stochastic independence, actual IQ measurement model, and covariance feasibility for arbitrary parameter choices remain outside this proof scope.
