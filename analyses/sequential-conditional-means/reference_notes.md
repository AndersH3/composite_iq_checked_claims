# Reference notes and theorem-to-claim map

Sources were accessed on **14 November 2026**. This register identifies the editions, author versions, and selected passages consulted for the mathematical claims in this analysis. It does not claim that every page of every cited book was read. The report supplies its own proofs and algebraic substitutions; references identify the established theorems on which those proofs rest.

Definitions, selected model parameters, and the assumption of a joint Gaussian test model are stated assumptions. A mathematical theorem does not establish that a real test battery follows that model. The IQ reporting scale `Q = 100 + 15 X`, the starting IQ 150, the correlation `3/5`, and the requested counts are the inputs to the worked examples.

## Proof techniques and applications to the report

- **Recursive constants and partial sums:** Hammack, Section 10.1, p. 182, gives the ordinary induction principle. The report verifies a base case and an inductive invariant for every prefix. The finite telescoping product is explicit algebra; its denominators are shown positive before cancellation.
- **One, two, or three equal starting scores:** these are substitutions into the proved general starting-block formulas, followed by algebra. They are not separately assumed probabilistic laws.
- **Population and conditional SDs:** population SD follows from the variance-covariance expansion. Conditional SD uses the Gaussian residual calculation. Standard deviation zero for a fully fixed recursively filled list follows from the definition of variance. These are different random quantities.
- **Singular endpoints:** positive-semidefinite covariance existence and the zero-variance implication justify the support restrictions. No inverse of a singular full covariance matrix is silently used.
- **IQ 150 examples:** the conversion to standardized `a = 10/3`, the mean transformation `100 + 15 B`, and SD multiplication by 15 use the cited affine rules. The table results are arithmetic substitutions at exact correlation `3/5`, with rounding applied only to displayed numbers. They are model calculations, not new participant observations.
- **Correlation-only counterexample:** its means, variances, covariance, and positive-probability conditional value are checked directly from its finite probability mass function. This counterexample limits the conditional-mean conclusion; it does not contradict the covariance-only population-variance identity.

## Verification scope

Source references support the stated mathematical foundations. The mathematical proofs in the report are written arguments conditional on the stated assumptions. Repository runtime/property tests, structural contract audits, and scoped symbolic checks have their own documented targets; they are not a machine-checked proof of this new exposition or an empirical proof of its statistical model. No source is cited as evidence that actual IQ-test data necessarily satisfy equicorrelation or joint Gaussianity.
