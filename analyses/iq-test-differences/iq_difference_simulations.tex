\chapter{Experimental design}
Each row represents one synthetic battery or one independent pair of people.
The default campaign comprises eight replicates of five million rows per
scenario, with blocks of 250,000 and four Dask threads. NumPy generates
worker-local arrays and Dask schedules tasks; only three centered-moment records per block
are returned. Each contains the sample count, mean, and sum of squared
centered deviations; shared pooling also retains between-block variation. A root SeedSequence allocates deterministic child streams.
This is reproducibility infrastructure, not a proof of PRNG independence.
Gaussian equicorrelation is generated by projection, including the singular
negative boundary. Two mixture models, the two-group covariance example,
the first-score-145 conditional model and ten-test sensitivity are included.
The block scenario measures a cross-group absolute gap (not the mean of all
ten pair gaps).
\chapter{Uncertainty and acceptance}
For iid rows with finite second moment, the unbiased sample variance is
$S^2=\sum_i(y_i-\bar y)^2/(n-1)$ by expanding squares and linearity
of expectation. The shared block reducer computes centered deviations and
merges them using the parallel-variance identity. For two blocks of sizes
$n_a,n_b$ and means $m_a,m_b$, the merged centered sum of squares is
\[M_2=M_{2,a}+M_{2,b}+\frac{n_a n_b}{n_a+n_b}(m_b-m_a)^2.\]
This follows by expanding each deviation around its block mean; the
linear terms sum to zero. Independence makes $\Var(\bar Y)=\Var(Y)/n$;
the estimated Monte Carlo standard error is $S/\sqrt n$.
The six-standard-error diagnostic plus a small absolute floating-point
tolerance is an engineering check, not a simultaneous confidence guarantee
or a proof. Degenerate cases have zero model variance.
Analytic values use SciPy quadrature for the order-statistic expectation
and the folded normal library for the conditional gap. The theoretical
formulas are established in the mathematical companion.

From this analysis directory, execute \texttt{make data} and
\texttt{make pdf}. The shared repository requirements specify the software
stack. The block function and analytical reference declare separate input-domain,
output-structure, finiteness, ordering and degeneracy contracts. Both the
block reducer and the parallel moment merger are reused directly from
\texttt{reporting/block\_simulations.py}.

remain separate from both the completed simulation and algebraic build. The report distinguishes its retained offline evidence from Dask campaign
