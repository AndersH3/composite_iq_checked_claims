\subsection{Specific observations in the recorded run}
At $n=1$ and $n=1000$, the observed factor-prediction MSEs are $0.400051$ and $6.663375\times10^{-4}$, respectively. The raw-mean SD at $n=1000$ is $0.774936$, close to its positive correlation floor $\sqrt{0.6}$; the decreasing MSE concerns recovery of the simulated factor, not removal of population differences. The pooled rare count ranges from 1 to 4 across the eight coupled sizes. A count on this scale is sensitive to individual extreme draws; the exact intervals quantify that uncertainty. The shared draws prevent adding these eight counts as independent evidence.
