\subsection{Specific observations in the recorded run}
The nominal E2 rule admits 134536 of 4000000 pairs ($3.3634$\%), while OR2 admits 79260 ($1.9815$\%). The latter is $2.643$ binomial standard errors from its fixed model target. The target is outside this run's displayed 95\% Wilson interval. Such a pointwise interval can miss a known target in a correctly specified simulation; the much more conservative, predeclared implementation diagnostic still passes. The cutoff and seeds were not retuned.

For Rare, 11 joint passes occur among 115 passers of A. Thus the conditional estimate is based on that small actual denominator, not the full 4000000 pairs. The conditional interval and the separate exact joint interval make the loss of rare-event precision visible.
