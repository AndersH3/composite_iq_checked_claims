\subsection{Specific observations in the recorded run}
The nominal E2 rule admits 1349058 of 40000000 pairs ($3.3726$\%), while OR2 admits 799161 ($1.9979$\%). The latter is $0.948$ binomial standard errors from its fixed model target. The target is inside this run's displayed 95\% Wilson interval. Such a pointwise interval can miss a known target in a correctly specified simulation; the much more conservative, predeclared implementation diagnostic still passes. The cutoff and seeds were not retuned.

For Rare, 96 joint passes occur among 1230 passers of A. Thus the conditional estimate is based on that small actual denominator, not the full 40000000 pairs. The conditional interval and the separate exact joint interval make the loss of rare-event precision visible.
